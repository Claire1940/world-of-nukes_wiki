<Section icon={Swords} title="How the Nuclear Strike System Works">

In World of Nukes, offensive power comes from two layered systems: conventional missile launchers and large-scale nuclear attack infrastructure. Both operate through a launch interface where you select an enemy target and confirm the strike. A proper World of Nukes nuclear bomb comparison therefore compares weapons by **unlock stage, strategic role, and attack pressure**, because the game presents systems and capabilities rather than a public damage table.

<Callout type="warning" title="No Fabricated Stats">
Official sources document strike systems, target selection, and unlock flow — not specific damage values or blast radii. This comparison ranks weapons by confirmed role and strategic use. For the exact values that apply to your setup, check the official Roblox game page and in-game descriptions.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step: Planning a Nuclear Strike">

A successful nuclear strike in World of Nukes is prepared long before you press launch. Follow this sequence to convert offense into a real advantage.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Build the Economy First">
    Missile infrastructure and nuclear systems demand heavy resources. Upgrade income-producing buildings before committing resources to offensive construction, so a single failed campaign does not cripple your island.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Construct and Activate Launch Infrastructure">
    Build the required missile structures and progress far enough to access nuclear attack systems. Confirm the launch interface works before an enemy appears at your border.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Scout the Target Island">
    Use jets or observation to identify the enemy's economic core and military buildings. Rank targets by value: income structures and missile infrastructure outrank outer decorations.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Secure Your Own Defenses">
    Place anti-air coverage and shields around your core before launching. Every offensive commitment opens a window where your island is a tempting counterattack target.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Launch and Follow Up">
    Confirm the target, launch the strike, and follow with jets or warships for sustained pressure while the enemy rebuilds. Then rebuild your own offensive capacity before the next operation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Combined Arms Wins">
The strongest attacks pair a nuclear strike with jets for speed and warships for sustained coastal pressure. A lone strike rarely ends a war — follow-up operations do.
</Callout>

</Section>
