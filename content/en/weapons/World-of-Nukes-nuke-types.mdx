<Section icon={Map} title="World of Nukes Nuke Types Overview">

In World of Nukes, offensive power is built around a layered missile and nuclear attack system. You construct missile infrastructure on your island, gain access to launch controls, and select enemy targets directly. Every nuke type in the game serves a distinct strategic purpose, and choosing the right one at the right moment separates successful attackers from players who waste resources.

The three core attack categories are the **missile launch system** for deliberate strikes, the **nuclear attack system** for large-scale pressure, and **target selection and launch control** for managing how and when you commit. Weapon effectiveness depends on your unlock stage, resource reserves, and whether your own island can survive retaliation.

<Section icon={Target} title="How to Launch: Step-by-Step">

Follow this launch workflow to avoid wasting offensive resources on poor targets.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Build Missile Infrastructure">
    Construct the missile-related structures your island needs. Treat this as a strategic investment once your resource base is stable, not an automatic early unlock.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Learn the Launch Interface">
    Familiarize yourself with the launch controls and enemy target selection before committing to any attack. Practice confirms range, availability, and weapon readiness.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Scout the Target Island">
    Review the enemy island and prioritize valuable structures. Avoid attacks that leave your own island undefended while your weapons are committed.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Confirm and Fire">
    Select the target, confirm the launch, and verify the strike. Coordinate with jets or warships if the target is heavily defended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Timing Matters">
Major nuclear strikes create the most value against high-value enemy targets at moments when the attack produces a decisive advantage. Patience beats spamming missiles.
</Callout>
