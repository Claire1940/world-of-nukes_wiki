In World of Nukes, the best nukes and missiles are not simply picked from a menu — they are unlocked through island progression, funded by your economy, and deployed through dedicated offensive structures. The game's attack system revolves around three pillars: the missile launch system, the nuclear attack system, and the launch control interface used to select enemy targets.

Weapon effectiveness depends on availability, unlock requirements, target value, and your ability to support attacks with a strong island economy and defenses. A player who rushes nuclear weapons with a weak economy will struggle far more than one who builds steady income first.

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: How to Launch Your First Nuke">

Launching your first strike in World of Nukes is a strategic decision, not an automatic action. Follow this sequence to make your earliest attacks count.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Build Missile Infrastructure">
    Construct the missile-related structures your island requires. These buildings gate access to the launch system, so prioritize them once your economy can comfortably fund them.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Learn the Launch Interface">
    Open the launch controls and explore the interface before committing to an attack. Understand how targets are displayed, how weapons are selected, and what confirmation steps exist.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Scout the Enemy Island">
    Review the target before launching. Identify economic structures, exposed defensive gaps, and the overall development level of the rival island.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Confirm Range and Resource Cost">
    Check weapon availability, range, and the resource cost of the strike. A wasted attack on a low-value target weakens your own position.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Launch and Reassess">
    Confirm the strike, then reassess your own defenses. Never leave your island undefended while your offensive resources are committed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Never Launch Unprepared">
Launching a strike while your own island lacks anti-air coverage and shields invites immediate retaliation. The strongest players treat every attack as a two-sided calculation: damage dealt versus exposure created.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Supporting Your Offense: Defense and Vehicles">

The best nukes lose their value if your own island crumbles to a counterattack. Every offensive plan in World of Nukes should be paired with defensive readiness and vehicle support.

**Defense pairing essentials:**

- **Anti-air defenses** placed around important buildings and likely attack routes blunt retaliatory strikes.
- **Island shields** protect the island core, preserving the infrastructure that funds your next launch.
- **Layered layouts** ensure a single enemy strike cannot wipe out all critical buildings at once.

**Vehicle support roles:**

| Vehicle | Role | Offensive Use | Defensive Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jets | Fast air attack | Rapid strikes on exposed structures | Mobile response to incoming threats |
| Warships | Naval pressure | Sustained coastal bombardment | Covering attack routes from the water |
| Combined force | Island assault | Jets open gaps, warships hold pressure | Covering multiple approach vectors |

<Callout type="tip" title="Scout Before You Commit">
Scout the target with jets before committing warships or a nuclear strike. Identifying where the enemy's shields and anti-air coverage sit lets you aim where the defense is thinnest, maximizing the value of every missile.
</Callout>
