<Section icon={Shield} title="Common Mistakes to Avoid">

Even experienced players waste resources on avoidable errors. These are the pitfalls our team sees most often in community discussions.

- **Spreading upgrades too thin** across multiple warhead classes
- **Ignoring cooldown stats** and focusing purely on damage numbers
- **Dismantling duplicate blueprints** before checking fusion requirements
- **Skipping daily material runs** early because rewards seem small
- **Launching at first sight** instead of waiting for enemy clusters to group

<Callout type="warning" title="Resource Warning">
Rare upgrade cores are the main bottleneck for every player. Verify the total cost to max your chosen S-Tier warhead before spending anything, and keep a reserve for future content updates.
</Callout>
