<Section icon={Rocket} title="How Nuclear Weapons Work in World of Nukes">

World of Nukes is a Roblox strategy tycoon built around nuclear warfare. You develop your island into a military nation, deploy missile systems, and launch offensive strikes against other players. Nuclear weapons represent the large-scale offensive tier of the game, sitting above conventional missile attacks in both impact and progression requirements.

<Callout type="warning" title="Note on Weapon Data">
Specific weapon names, damage values, and unlock costs have not been officially documented in detail. This guide covers the confirmed attack systems and strategic principles without inventing statistics. Always verify current numbers in-game.
</Callout>

The offensive system operates on three connected levels:

| Attack Layer | Role | What It Involves | Unlock Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Missile Launch System | Core offense | Launch interface, enemy target selection, resource cost | Build required missile infrastructure |
| Nuclear Attack System | Strategic strikes | Large-scale strikes creating island-wide pressure | Progress to nuclear attack infrastructure |
| Launch Control | Attack management | Target review, confirmation, weapon availability | Access active launch controls via offensive structures |

Weapon effectiveness depends on four factors working together: availability of the weapon, your progression level, the value of the target, and whether your island economy and defenses can sustain repeated attacks.

<Section icon={Target} title="Launching a Nuclear Strike: Step by Step">

A nuclear strike in World of Nukes should always be a deliberate strategic decision, not an automatic action. Follow this sequence to avoid wasting offensive resources.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Build the Missile Infrastructure">
    Construct and activate the missile-related structures your island requires. These buildings unlock the launch interface and determine which attack options are available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Learn the Launch Interface">
    Open the launch controls and practice the target-selection flow before committing resources. Understand range, availability, and cost for each weapon option you can field.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Scout the Enemy Island">
    Review the target before launching. Identify exposed economic structures, poorly defended sections, and high-value military buildings worth the strike.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Confirm and Launch">
    Select the target, confirm the launch, and monitor the result. Larger nuclear strikes should be reserved for moments when the attack creates a decisive advantage.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Reassess Your Defenses">
    After any major strike, check that your own island remains protected. Attacks often trigger retaliation, so keep anti-air coverage and shields ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Reserve your largest strikes for developed rival nations with dense economic clusters. A nuclear weapon spent on a weak, scattered island rarely returns enough value to justify the resource cost.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Supporting Your Arsenal: Economy and Defense">

Nuclear weapons are only as strong as the island behind them. Players who rush offensive builds without an economy cannot sustain attacks, and players who attack without defense lose everything to retaliation.

Your progression should follow this priority order:

| Stage | Focus | Why It Matters for Nuclear Attacks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Core settlement and income | Funds all missile construction and launch costs |
| 2 | Economy upgrades | Enables repeated strikes and faster recovery |
| 3 | Infrastructure expansion | Unlocks advanced offensive construction |
| 4 | Defense and military power | Protects your arsenal from enemy strikes |
| 5 | Advanced structures and wonders | Endgame systems including the Doomsday Device |

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Mistake">
Do not spend all your resources on military assets early. An island with powerful weapons but a weak economy cannot rebuild after a counterattack, making your arsenal a one-time threat.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Endgame Weapons: The Doomsday Device">

At the highest progression tier, World of Nukes offers the Doomsday Device, a late-game strategic system connected to the ultimate military progression path. Like wonders, it represents the endpoint of sustained island development rather than a quick unlock.

| Endgame System | What It Is | Unlock Path | Strategic Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wonders | Advanced landmark structures | Core development, advanced construction access | Visible endgame objective, strategic specialization |
| Doomsday Device | Ultimate strategic weapon system | Higher-tier progression, endgame infrastructure | Peak military progression beyond standard vehicles |

Both systems demand the same foundation: reliable income, upgraded core infrastructure, and defenses strong enough to protect a high-value target. Once completed, these structures become priority targets for rivals, so defensive readiness must grow alongside them.

<Callout type="success" title="Endgame Rule">
Commit to wonders and the Doomsday Device only after your economy and defenses can support them. Advanced construction should serve a clear strategic objective, not simply fill available space.
</Callout>
