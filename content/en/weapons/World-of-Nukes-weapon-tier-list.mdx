<Section icon={Trophy} title="S-Tier and A-Tier Weapons">

<Section icon={Target} title="Mid and Low Tier Weapons Explained">

B-Tier weapons carry you reliably through the mid-game. They are cheap to maintain and easy to obtain, but their damage scaling falls off sharply once you reach the higher-level zones where enemies have significantly larger health pools.

C and D-Tier weapons mostly serve as stepping stones. Keep one equipped only until your first A-Tier option unlocks, then retire it. Their main value is completing collection rewards and daily quests that require specific weapon types.

Dedicate your primary slot to a blast-radius monster for PvE and your secondary to a fast, precise weapon for PvP. This covers both game modes without compromise.
