<Section icon={Map} title="World of Nukes Map Overview">

The World of Nukes map is built around a post-nuclear wasteland where safety and opportunity exist on opposite ends of the same road. The spawn region is deliberately forgiving: enemies are weak, supplies are common, and vendors are close together. As you push outward, the terrain changes, radiation increases, and the quality of loot climbs sharply.

Understanding the overall layout is the first skill every survivor should master. Instead of wandering aimlessly, treat the map as a series of concentric rings, each with its own threat level, resource types, and progression requirements.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Key Locations and What They Offer">

Every major location on the World of Nukes map serves a purpose, whether it is trading, healing, crafting, or high-risk looting. Learning which location answers which need saves hours of backtracking.

| Location Type | Primary Function | Threat Level | Recommended Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settlements | Vendors, quests, storage | Safe | All levels |
| Ruined towns | Loot, scrap farming | Low-Medium | 5+ |
| Military outposts | Weapons, ammo caches | Medium-High | 15+ |
| Bunkers | Rare gear, boss encounters | High | 25+ |
| Crater zones | Unique materials, radiation | Very High | 30+ |

<Callout type="warning" title="Radiation Warning">
Crater and blast zones inflict continuous radiation damage. Without proper protective equipment or consumable antidotes, even high-level players can be downed in seconds. Always carry anti-radiation supplies when heading into these regions.
</Callout>

Beyond the table above, keep these navigation facts in mind:

- **Settlements** double as respawn anchors, so discovering each one effectively extends your safety net across the map.
- **Ruined towns** restock loot on a timer, making them reliable recurring farm stops rather than one-time visits.
- **Bunkers** often contain locked doors that require keys found elsewhere on the map, encouraging looped exploration routes.
- **Crater zones** frequently host the rarest crafting materials, which is why endgame players accept the radiation risk.

<Section icon={Compass} title="Step-by-Step: Mapping the Wasteland Efficiently">

A structured exploration order turns the map from a hazard into a roadmap. Follow this sequence when starting a fresh character or relearning the terrain after an update.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Secure the Spawn Region">
    Complete starter quests, stockpile food and water, and fully explore the immediate area around spawn before crossing into the mid wasteland. A solid supply base prevents forced retreats later.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Discover All Settlements">
    Prioritize finding every settlement on the map. Each one unlocks fast travel, vendors, and a respawn point, which permanently improves every future run.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Establish Farm Routes">
    Link 3-4 ruined towns into a repeating loop that passes through a settlement for selling and restocking. Efficient loops beat random looting every time.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Gear Up for Bunkers">
    Use farm route income to upgrade weapons and armor until you can handle military outposts, then use outpost loot to prepare for bunker runs.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Conquer Radiation Zones">
    Only enter crater zones once you have reliable radiation protection and healing supplies. These areas are the final tier of map progression.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Route Efficiency">
Players who unlock all fast travel points early typically cut their total travel time by more than half across a full playthrough. Discovery is an investment that pays for itself.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Callout type="info" title="Reading the Table">
Farm value reflects repeat visits, not one-time clears. Bunkers offer the best single-run loot, but ruined towns often outperform them over an hour of looping because enemies are easier and travel is shorter.
</Callout>

Your ideal farming location also depends on your current goal:

- **Earning currency fast:** loop ruined towns near a settlement for quick sell trips.
- **Upgrading weapons:** target military outposts for ammo and weapon drops.
- **Chasing rare gear:** bunker runs offer the highest ceiling, at the highest risk.
- **Crafting endgame items:** crater materials are mandatory, so plan protection first.
