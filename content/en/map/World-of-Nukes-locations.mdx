<Section icon={Map} title="Understanding World of Nukes Locations">

In World of Nukes, locations are not a static map you explore — they are player-built islands scattered across the server's waters. Every match places you and up to six other players on separate islands, and every strategic decision about location comes down to three zones: **your island layout**, the **surrounding waters** where warships operate, and the **airspace** that jets use to reach rival islands.

Because the game is a PvP strategy tycoon, rival locations are dynamic. A nearby enemy island can become a nuclear threat within minutes, which makes early awareness of your neighbors one of the most important survival skills in the game.

<Callout type="warning" title="Server Population Matters">
Each server holds a maximum of 7 players. Fewer players means safer expansion time; a full server means rival islands are closer and enemy attacks can arrive sooner. Check who is online before committing resources to long construction projects.
</Callout>

### The Three Location Zones

| Zone | Primary Use | Key Structures | Risk Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Island Core | Economy, shields, critical buildings | Income buildings, Island Shield | Low (best protected) |
| Island Perimeter | Defense and military systems | Anti-air defenses, missile launchers | Medium |
| Open Waters & Airspace | Attacks on rival islands | Warships, jets | High (enemy contact) |

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Your Island Layout: Where to Place Everything">

Your own island is the location you control completely, and its internal layout determines how well you survive. Follow a layered pattern: the most valuable buildings in the center, defenses around them, and military systems positioned where they can respond quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Secure the Core">
    Place your first camp and income-producing structures centrally. A compact start keeps the island core protected and leaves room for organized outward expansion.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Expand Outward in a Pattern">
    Build additional structures in organized rings instead of scattering them. Reserve dedicated open space for missile systems, anti-air coverage, and shields before the island fills up.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Ring the Core With Defenses">
    Position anti-air defenses where their coverage protects the central base, resource structures, and likely attack paths. Layer outer coverage for exposed areas.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Position Offensive Systems">
    Place missile infrastructure where it is functional but not the first thing an enemy strike destroys. Balance attack readiness against protecting your economy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Placement Priority">
Protect the island core before extending defenses to less important outer structures. Losing an outer building hurts far less than losing your income base or shield.
</Callout>

### Island Placement Reference

| Structure Type | Recommended Location | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income buildings | Center, behind shields | Hardest for enemy strikes to eliminate |
| Island Shield | Core, covering top-value buildings | Absorbs nuclear pressure on your investments |
| Anti-air defenses | Perimeter and attack routes | Intercepts incoming threats before they reach the core |
| Missile launchers | Mid-ring, defended but accessible | Ready to fire without being an easy first target |
| Future expansion space | Outer edges, pre-planned | Keeps later upgrades from forcing rebuilds |

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Rival Island Locations: Scouting and Target Selection">

Every rival island is a potential target — and a potential threat. Attack effectiveness in World of Nukes depends heavily on choosing the right target location at the right time.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="High-Value Targets">
    - **Economy structures** — cripple enemy income
    - **Unshielded cores** — maximum strike impact
    - **Defense clusters** — open the island to follow-ups
  </Card>
  <Card title="Low-Value Targets">
    - Outer filler buildings
    - Heavily shielded positions
    - Empty or abandoned islands
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timing Windows">
    - After an enemy attack (rebuilding phase)
    - When defenses are under construction
    - When the player is offline
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Before launching any strike, review the enemy island first. Confirm range and weapon availability, prioritize valuable targets, and never launch an attack that leaves your own island undefended. Treat missile deployment as a strategic decision rather than an automatic action.

<Callout type="info" title="Combined Assaults Work Best">
Scout the target before committing your strongest vehicles. Use jets for speed and warships for sustained pressure, and prioritize economic and strategic structures during the assault.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Attack Routes: Air and Naval Locations">

Jets and warships reach rival islands through different locations, and each system favors different routes.

| Vehicle | Route Type | Strengths | Best Target Locations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jets | Airspace, direct approach | Speed, flexible attack angles | Exposed structures, undefended flanks |
| Warships | Open and coastal waters | Durability, sustained pressure | Coastal buildings, prolonged sieges |
| Combined force | Air plus naval | Creates multiple attack vectors | Developed, well-defended islands |

**Jet tactics:** use speed to control the distance from enemy defenses, strike vulnerable targets instead of grinding against protected positions, and return to a safe position after each attack run.

**Warship tactics:** maintain naval positioning around enemy islands to apply steady pressure, coordinate with air operations, and avoid exposing the ship when the target is heavily defended.

<Callout type="warning" title="Keep a Defense Force at Home">
Maintain a defensive response force instead of committing every vehicle offensively. Use air and naval units to cover different attack routes toward your own island, and rebuild losses before launching another major operation.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Endgame Locations: Wonders and the Doomsday Device">

Late-game structures change your island's location priorities. Wonders and the Doomsday Device are high-value construction sites that attract enemy attention, so their placement and protection become a core strategic concern.

- **Wonders** are long-term construction objectives for developed islands — treat them as strategic investments placed within your shielded core.
- **The Doomsday Device** represents the ultimate military progression and must be defended like your most important building.
- **Both** create high-value targets, so upgrade your defensive capacity before you start building them.
