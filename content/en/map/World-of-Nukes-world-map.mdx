<Section icon={Target} title="Global Test Sites: Where the Map Lights Up">

From Nevada to Kazakhstan, from the Arctic to the Pacific, nuclear testing has spread across the planet. More than **2,000 nuclear tests** have been conducted worldwide, leaving a distinctive geographic signature on the world map.

</Callout>
