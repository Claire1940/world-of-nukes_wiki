<Section icon={Map} title="Understanding the World of Nukes Map">

In World of Nukes, the "map" is your island and the rival islands surrounding it. Every server holds up to **7 players**, each developing their own island into a military nation. How you organize space — economy, missiles, defenses, and vehicle access — determines whether your country thrives or gets flattened in the first exchange of strikes.

Think of your island as four functional zones. Each zone has a purpose, and keeping them separated makes your base easier to defend, upgrade, and expand.

| Zone | Primary Purpose | Key Structures | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Economy Core** | Income and progression | Resource-generating buildings | ★★★★★ |
| **Military Zone** | Offensive strikes | Missile launch systems, nuclear infrastructure | ★★★★ |
| **Defense Ring** | Protection from attacks | Anti-air defenses, shields | ★★★★★ |
| **Expansion Border** | Future growth | Reserved open space | ★★★ |

<Callout type="tip" title="Layout Principle">
Keep the initial layout compact. A scattered base wastes space, dilutes anti-air coverage, and makes rebuilding after an attack far more painful than it needs to be.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Island Layout: Step-by-Step Map Planning">

Follow this progression to turn a small camp into a defensible military nation. The order matters — building missiles before your economy can sustain them is the most common beginner mistake.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the First Camp">
    Place your initial camp and organize the available island space. Prioritize resource-generating structures first, and keep the footprint tight so later expansion stays efficient.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Expand in an Organized Pattern">
    Use collected resources to construct additional buildings, expanding outward deliberately instead of scattering structures. Reserve open space for missile systems, anti-air defenses, and shields.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Deploy Missile Systems">
    Build missile-related structures once resources allow. Learn the launch interface before committing to an attack, and select enemy targets carefully — offensive resources are limited.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Early Defenses">
    Place anti-air defenses around important buildings and likely attack routes. Use shields to protect the island core, and keep growing the economy so defensive coverage expands with the country.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Progress Toward Advanced Structures">
    Once income and defenses are stable, commit resources to wonders and endgame systems like the Doomsday Device as part of a clear strategic plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Avoid spending everything on military assets early. A broken economy cannot fund rebuilds, replacements, or the upgrades needed to survive repeated enemy pressure.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Defense Placement and Map Zones">

Defense in World of Nukes protects the island's most important structures first, then expands outward as the country grows. A layered layout means one attack never wipes out everything you have built.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Anti-Air Defense">
    - **Intercepts incoming aerial attacks**
    - Position near core buildings and attack paths
    - Sustains coverage under repeated pressure
  </Card>
  <Card title="Island Shield">
    - **Protects the core from nuclear strikes**
    - Preserves critical buildings during offensives
    - Grows more valuable as investment grows
  </Card>
  <Card title="Layered Layout">
    - **Inner protection for the core**
    - Outer anti-air coverage for exposed areas
    - Reserved space for future upgrades
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

| Defense Layer | What It Protects | Placement Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inner shield | Economy core, critical buildings | Close to the most valuable island section |
| Mid anti-air ring | Military and production buildings | Around central base and attack routes |
| Outer coverage | Exposed expansion areas | Extend only after the core is secure |

<Callout type="success" title="Survival Rule">
Protect the island core before extending defenses to less important areas. A defended core can rebuild a lost border; a lost core ends the game.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Attacking Rival Islands: Map Movement">

The map around your island is where offense happens. Jets and warships approach enemy islands from different angles, and combining them creates openings that neither achieves alone.

| Vehicle | Role | Best Use | Tactical Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Jets** | Fast air attack | Rapid strikes, exposed targets | Control distance, retreat after runs |
| **Warships** | Naval pressure | Sustained coastal attacks | Coordinate with air operations |
| **Combined assault** | Full island attack | Developed rival nations | Scout first, prioritize economic targets |

Key routing principles:

- Scout the target island with fast jets before committing your strongest vehicles
- Attack vulnerable structures instead of grinding against heavily defended positions
- Maintain a defensive response force at home instead of sending everything out
- Prioritize economic and strategic structures during an assault to cripple rival recovery

<Callout type="tip" title="Attack Timing">
Reserve major nuclear strikes for high-value enemy targets and moments when the attack creates a decisive advantage — not for opportunistic potshots.
</Callout>

</Section>
