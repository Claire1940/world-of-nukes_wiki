<Section icon={Map} title="Nuclear Explosion Basics in World of Nukes">

The **World of Nukes nuclear explosion** system is the heart of the game. When a warhead detonates, the simulation divides the affected area into concentric damage zones, each with its own destruction profile. Understanding these zones is the difference between wiping out a high-value target and wasting an expensive warhead on empty terrain.

Detonation outcomes depend on three variables: the **warhead yield class**, the **detonation altitude** (ground burst versus air burst), and the **target environment** (dense cities, military installations, or open land). Players who learn how these variables interact can plan strikes with precision instead of guessing.

**Key Explosion Variables:**
- Yield class (kiloton to multi-megaton range)
- Detonation altitude and burst type
- Target density and structural hardening
- Terrain features that channel or block blast effects

<Callout type="tip" title="Editor Tip">
Before committing a warhead, scout the target. A high-yield strike on scattered rural targets wastes most of the blast radius on empty ground, while low-yield strikes against hardened bunkers may barely scratch them.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Blast Zone Breakdown and Damage Zones">

Every detonation in World of Nukes generates a layered set of damage zones. Each zone shrinks or expands based on the yield class you deploy, so knowing the layer order helps you estimate outcomes at a glance.

| Blast Zone | Relative Radius | Damage Effect | Best Used Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fireball | Smallest, innermost | Total vaporization, nothing survives | Command centers, hard bunkers |
| Shockwave | Medium | Structural collapse, building destruction | Cities, industrial districts |
| Thermal Radiation | Large | Fires, burns, secondary ignition | Sprawling urban areas |
| Fallout Zone | Largest (ground bursts) | Lingering area denial, residual damage | Blocking enemy reinforcement routes |

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch the Fallout">
Fallout zones persist after the initial flash. If your strategy depends on occupying or moving forces through the region afterward, ground bursts can deny the area to you as much as to your opponent.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Step-by-Step: Planning a Perfect Strike">

Follow this workflow every time you commit a warhead. Rushing the targeting phase is the most common way players waste resources.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Scout and Value the Target">
    Identify what sits inside the projected blast zones. Tag high-value structures such as command posts, factories, and population centers, and estimate their durability.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Select the Yield Class">
    Match warhead size to target value. If the fireball and shockwave zones already cover every key structure, a larger yield adds cost without adding destruction.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Choose Burst Type">
    Pick an air burst for maximum structural damage across a wide area, or a ground burst when fallout and area denial matter more than raw destruction.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Set the Detonation Point">
    Position the blast so the highest-value cluster sits near the center of the shockwave zone, not the edge, to guarantee destruction instead of partial damage.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Follow Up">
    After the strike, reassess the map. Damaged-but-standing targets may be finished with a cheap tactical warhead rather than another full-scale launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Strike Checklist Passed">
A well-planned detonation destroys the target cluster, preserves your resource pool, and leaves your reload cycle free for the next threat. If any of those three fail, review your yield choice before the next launch.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Common Mistakes and Checklist">

Most failed strikes trace back to the same handful of errors. Run through this checklist before every launch to catch them early.

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Oversized yield on sparse targets | Wasted resources, long cooldown | Downgrade one yield class and re-target |
| Ground burst on land you need | Fallout denies your own advance | Switch to air burst |
| Target on zone edge | Partial damage, target survives | Center the shockwave on key structures |
| Ignoring enemy interception | Warhead lost before detonation | Suppress defenses or bait interceptors first |
