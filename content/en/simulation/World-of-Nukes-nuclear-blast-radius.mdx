<Section icon={Crosshair} title="How Nuclear Blast Coverage Works">

Understanding the **nuclear blast radius** in World of Nukes is the foundation of both attacking and defending effectively. Every offensive weapon in the game delivers damage across a defined coverage zone around the point you select. Standard missile systems focus destruction on a tighter footprint, while the nuclear attack system applies far broader, island-wide pressure against high-value targets.

Coverage interacts with three practical factors:

- **Weapon tier**: larger strategic weapons threaten more structures per strike than tactical missiles.
- **Target layout**: dense island cores absorb more total damage than scattered layouts.
- **Defensive layers**: shields and anti-air reduce how much of the blast zone converts into real damage.

<Callout type="warning" title="Data Note">
Exact blast radius values per weapon have not been officially documented. This guide explains the confirmed strike system — launch interface, target selection, and strategic role — without inventing specific radius numbers. Check the official Roblox game page for the latest weapon details.
</Callout>

</Section>

Follow this launch workflow to get the most out of every attack's coverage zone.

  <Step num="1" title="Build Offensive Infrastructure">

  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Scout the Target Island">

  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Confirm Range and Cost">

  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Select High-Value Targets">

  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Launch and Reassess">
    Confirm the launch, observe the result, and never leave your own island undefended while your offensive resources are committed elsewhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Defending Against Blast Radius">

Blast coverage is only as dangerous as your defense is weak. A layered layout shrinks the effective damage of any incoming strike.

| Defense Layer | What It Counters | Placement Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anti-Air Defense | Incoming aerial attacks and missiles | Around the central base, resources, and likely attack paths |
| Island Shield | Large-scale nuclear strikes | Close to the most valuable island section |
| Layout Spacing | Single-strike total destruction | Reserve open space so one blast cannot wipe the core |
| Defensive Vehicles | Follow-up and repeat attacks | Keep a response force home instead of full commitment |

<Callout type="tip" title="Core-First Rule">
Protect the island core before extending coverage to outer structures. Losing peripheral buildings is recoverable; losing your economic heart to one nuclear strike is not.
</Callout>

</Section>
