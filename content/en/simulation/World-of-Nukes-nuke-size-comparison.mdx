A useful World of Nukes nuke size comparison is therefore built from three verifiable factors: the weapon category, its unlock requirements, and the strategic scope of the strike it produces. | Comparison Factor | Conventional Missile | Nuclear Strike |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Selected enemy targets | Island-wide attack pressure |

| Best use | Precise, low-cost attacks | High-value decisive targets |
| Resource cost | Lower | Higher |

<Section icon={Shield} title="How Defense Shrinks Effective Strike Size">

A strike's effective size is not fixed—enemy defenses reduce it. When comparing nuke sizes, always factor in what the target's defenses will absorb. Anti-air defenses intercept incoming attacks, while shields add a protective layer over the island core against large-scale nuclear strikes.

| Defense Layer | Counters | Effect on Strike Size | Placement Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anti-air defense | Aerial and missile attacks | Reduces repeat strike impact | Near core and attack routes |
| Island shield | Nuclear strikes | Blunts large-scale damage | Over highest-value section |
| Layered layout | All attack types | Prevents total loss in one hit | Inner core, then outer ring |

  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Match Tier to Target">

  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Confirm Launch Controls">

  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Secure Your Own Island">

  </Step>

<Section icon={Target} title="Target Value and Strike Size Matching">

The core principle of nuke size comparison is matching strike scale to target value. A high-value target—a developed economy, missile infrastructure, or an endgame project like a Wonder or the Doomsday Device—justifies a larger strike. Scattered low-tier buildings rarely do.

| Target Type | Value Level | Recommended Strike Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income buildings | High | Strategic | Cripples enemy recovery |
| Missile infrastructure | High | Strategic or Nuclear | Removes retaliation threat |
| Wonders and Doomsday Device | Very high | Nuclear (if defended) | Must be protected on your own island too |
| Outer low-tier buildings | Low | Tactical | Not worth heavy investment |
