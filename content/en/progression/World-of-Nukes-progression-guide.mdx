- **World of Nukes progression** hinges on smart resource spending, not grinding speed.
- **Early game**: Focus on one core squad and clear campaign stages for permanent rewards.
- **Mid game**: Shift priorities toward gear quality, faction synergy, and event currency.

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Progression Path">

Follow this sequence when starting a fresh account or resetting your priorities. The order matters: skipping ahead usually creates resource deficits that take weeks to repair.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Complete the Tutorial and Early Campaign">
    Push the campaign as far as possible before touching any other mode. Early stages refund their cost through first-clear rewards, and they unlock the systems you will need later. Do not reroll endlessly; a solid start matters more than a perfect one.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Lock In a Core Team of Five">
    Choose one tank, one or two damage dealers, and two support units. Commit your upgrade materials to these five only. A fully built core team clears far more content than a roster of half-leveled units.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Clear Every Daily and Weekly Task">
    Dailies are the backbone of long-term progression. Missions, logins, and activity points compound over weeks. Treat your daily reset as a non-negotiable checkpoint, even on short play sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Join an Active Alliance Early">
    Alliance membership provides passive income, shared shop currency, and group content access. An average active group beats an inactive top-ranked one for practical progression.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Transition to Gear and Endgame Optimization">
    Once your core team hits level walls, shift spending toward gear enhancement and secondary stat rolls. This is where mid-game power actually comes from, not raw unit levels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Do not level every new unit you unlock "just to try them." Experimental builds should wait until your core five are fully invested. Early experimental spending is the number one cause of stuck accounts.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Resource Management Priorities">

Resources in World of Nukes are time-gated, so allocation discipline separates fast progressers from stuck ones. Rank the value of each currency before spending it.

| Resource | Best Use | Avoid Spending On | Refresh Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | Core team gear enhancement | Random low-tier units | Medium |
| EXP materials | Main five units only | Benchwarmers | High |
| Pull currency | Rate-up banners for key units | Standard pool when rate-up is live | High |
| Arena tokens | Shop-exclusive gear fragments | Impulse cosmetic buys | Medium |
| Event currency | Limited-time units and rare mats | Common farmable items | Highest during events |

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Spend on Rate-Ups">
    - **Banners with boosted odds** offer the best expected value
    - Save currency between banners if none are relevant
    - Event units often define mid-game power spikes
  </Card>
  <Card title="Farm What Is Scarce">
    - Identify your current bottleneck weekly
    - Route stamina toward that single deficit
    - Gear materials usually matter more than unit EXP after mid-game
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bank Event Currency">
    - **Limited events** offer the best return per point
    - Unspent event tokens often convert poorly after the event ends
    - Prioritize exclusive items over farmable commons
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Callout type="success" title="Weekly Audit Habit">
Once a week, check where your resources actually went. If more than a third of your spending went to non-core units or non-essential shop items, tighten up the following week. Small leaks compound into weeks of lost progress.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Team Building and Combat Fundamentals">

A functional team composition beats a collection of strong units. Balance roles first, then refine synergy.

| Role | Function | Ideal Count | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tank | Absorbs damage, protects backline | 1 | Prioritize survivability stats |
| Main DPS | Primary damage output | 1-2 | Focus offensive stats fully |
| Support/Healer | Sustain and buffs | 1-2 | Often the difference in long fights |
| Utility/Debuffer | Crowd control, enemy weaken | 0-1 | Situational, valuable in hard content |

<Callout type="tip" title="Role Coverage First">
Before chasing rarity tiers, confirm your squad covers tanking, healing, and damage. A balanced common-rarity team clears more stages than an unbalanced team of rare units.
</Callout>

For difficult stages, adjust your approach instead of brute-forcing levels:

- Retry with a different ability order before spending upgrade materials.
- Reposition or swap one unit to counter the enemy composition.
- Check whether the stage has a mechanic (enrage timers, shields, waves) that rewards a specific counter.
- Only invest resources once you have confirmed the wall is a stats issue, not a strategy issue.
