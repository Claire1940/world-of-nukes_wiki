<Callout type="info" title="Quick Guide">
- **World of Nukes upgrades** follow one rule: economy first, military second.
- **Income buildings** should be upgraded before any offensive or cosmetic structures.
- **Anti-air defenses and shields** protect your investment before you create high-value targets.
- **Wonders and the Doomsday Device** are endgame projects that require a stable economy and defenses.
- **Jets and warships** unlock flexible attack options once your island can fund and defend them.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Map} title="Upgrade Priorities: What to Upgrade First">

In World of Nukes, every Robux-free player lives or dies by their upgrade order. An island that rushes missiles before income collapses the moment an enemy strikes back. An island that builds a stable economy first can recover from attacks, fund defenses, and eventually out-produce rivals.

The core upgrade philosophy is simple: **each upgrade should either increase income or protect the income you already have**. Everything else — missiles, jets, warships, wonders — is built on top of that foundation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Income buildings | 1st | Immediately | Slow growth, no recovery funds |
| Anti-air defenses | 2nd | Before first attacks | Loss of key structures |

| Missile systems | 3rd | Economy stable | No offensive pressure |

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Players who spend everything on missile systems early often lose their economy to a single counterattack. Without income, you cannot rebuild — and without rebuilding, you cannot keep attacking.

<Section icon={Shield} title="Economy and Defense Upgrades">

Your economy is the engine of every other upgrade. Upgrade income-producing buildings before optional structures, and reinvest earnings into buildings that improve long-term growth instead of stockpiling cash.

Defense upgrades come in two layers:

| Defense Layer | Role | Placement | Upgrade Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anti-air defenses | Intercept incoming aerial attacks | Near core buildings and likely attack paths | Coverage of central base and resource structures |
| Island shield | Protects core from nuclear strikes | Close to the most valuable island section | Protect critical buildings during offensives |
| Layered layout | Combines both systems | Inner core protection, outer anti-air ring | Expand the network as the country grows |

**Defensive upgrade rules:**

- Protect the island core before extending coverage to outer structures.
- Place anti-air where coverage overlaps resource buildings and military infrastructure.
- Reserve space in your layout for future defensive upgrades — do not box yourself in.
- Rebuild losses before launching another major offensive operation.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip">
A layered defense reduces the risk of losing all important buildings to a single attack. Inner protection covers the core and key buildings; outer anti-air coverage handles exposed areas.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Upgrade Path">

Follow this progression path to grow a small camp into a military nation without stalling.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the Core Settlement">
    Place the initial camp and organize the available island space. Secure a reliable income source, build the basic structures required for progression, and keep the layout compact so later expansion stays efficient.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Strengthen the Economy">
    Upgrade income-producing buildings first and reinvest earnings into long-term growth. Avoid spending all resources on military assets too early — a stable economy lets you recover faster after attacks.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Expand Infrastructure">
    Unlock additional construction options and organize production, utility, and defensive buildings in an organized outward pattern instead of scattering structures. Reserve open space for missile systems, anti-air defenses, and shields.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Upgrade Defense and Military Power">
    Once the economy is stable, invest in anti-air defenses, shields, and missile infrastructure. Prepare defenses before launching major attacks, and balance defensive spending with offensive expansion.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Progress Toward Advanced Structures">
    Save resources for high-impact structures — wonders and the Doomsday Device — and coordinate them with your preferred military strategy. Advanced construction should serve a clear objective, not fill space.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Milestone Check">
When your income can fund a full rebuild after a heavy attack without stalling your other upgrades, your economy is ready to support serious military investment.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Gamepass Upgrades Worth Considering">

Beyond in-game upgrades, World of Nukes offers gamepasses that improve convenience, awareness, and recovery speed. These are optional — the game is fully playable without them — but they complement specific playstyles.

| Gamepass | Benefit | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| VIP | VIP-related advantages and recognition | Players wanting an enhanced experience |
| Offline Tycoon | Supports progression while away | Players focused on steady economic growth |
| Early Warning Radar | Earlier awareness of incoming threats | Players who prioritize defense and reaction time |
| Quick Rebuild | Faster recovery from attacks | Players in frequent island warfare |

For current prices, check the Roblox experience store directly, as listings and prices may change with updates.

<Callout type="info" title="Value Note">
Players who get attacked often benefit most from **Early Warning Radar** and **Quick Rebuild**, while economic players get more value from **Offline Tycoon**.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Endgame Upgrade Goals">

Wonders and the Doomsday Device represent the highest tier of island progression. They are long-term strategic investments, not early-game purchases.

**Endgame progression objectives:**

- Maintain reliable income before beginning any major project.
- Upgrade defensive capacity before creating high-value targets on your island.
- Use jets and warships to defend the island and pressure rivals.
- Complete advanced construction as part of a broader military strategy.

| Endgame System | Unlock Requirement | Strategic Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Wonders | Advanced construction stage, sufficient resources, supporting infrastructure | Major advanced structure, visible progression objective |
| Doomsday Device | Higher-tier progression, endgame infrastructure, defenses to protect it | Endgame strategic objective, expands military progression |
