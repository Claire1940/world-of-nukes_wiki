<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Farming Your Way to Better Nukes">

Follow this progression path to climb the weapon tiers efficiently in a single session or across multiple runs.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Secure Your Starting Income">
    Deploy uranium mines and basic money-generating buildings early. Keep them clustered near your core so you can defend everything at once instead of chasing raiders across the map.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Hunt Airdrops and Stashes">
    Watch the map for airdrops and exposed enemy stashes. A single hot stash can inject hundreds into your balance — enough to skip an entire weapon tier.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Raid Weak Islands">
    Target players with unguarded money buildings. Anything you destroy generates payouts, and looted stashes compound your income faster than passive farming alone.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinvest in Better Weapons">
    Do not hoard cash. Buy the guided missile or bunker buster as soon as you can afford it — superior firepower lets you win fights against richer players and take their stashes.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Scale to Naval Weapons">
    Once your mines and buildings are generating consistently, save for the destroyer and cruiser. These top-tier units cement your lead and make your island nearly unraidable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Neglect Defense">
Players holding large stashes become prime targets. Reinvest in defensive weapons before your balance grows too attractive, or you will lose everything to a single coordinated raid.
</Callout>

</Section>
