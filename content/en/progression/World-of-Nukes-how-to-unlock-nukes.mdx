<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Unlock Path">

Follow this sequence in each war match to reach nuke-tier firepower as quickly as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish Your Island Position">
    Spawn into the island map and claim a defensible spot. Place your early structures close together so bunker clusters can cover them, and note where your stash sits — enemies will hunt it.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Build Early Economy">
    Use starting cash on income-generating structures. Money is the gate for every unlock tier, so prioritize anything that generates passive cash before buying luxury weapons.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Raid Stashes and Claim Airdrops">
    Destroy enemy players and take their stashes. Watch for airdrops landing on the map — claiming them can inject a large cash spike that skips entire unlock tiers.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Scale Uranium Mines">
    Uranium mines are the backbone of the late game. Stack as many as your territory supports; they fund guided missiles, destroyers, and cruisers without slowing your combat spending.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Unlock Heavy Ordnance">
    Once income is stable, purchase guided missiles and naval units. Use helicopters or planes for mobility, intercept attackers, and close out the match for the victory payout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Protect Your Progress">
Other players actively target your stash because it is worth millions. If your PC and stash fall, you lose accumulated value — reinforce with bunker clusters before pushing offense.
</Callout>

</Section>
