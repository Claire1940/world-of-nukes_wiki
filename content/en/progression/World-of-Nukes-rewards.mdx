<Section icon={Trophy} title="World of Nukes Rewards Overview">

The Nuka-World on Tour content drop introduced over 80 new rewards and items, spread across four public events, a five-tier prize terminal, and two dedicated vendors. Whether you are chasing unique weapons, camp decorations, or the classic Nuka arcade experience, understanding where each reward drops is the key to efficient farming.

Rewards fall into four categories:

- **Event-specific drops** — earned from Spin the Wheel, Most Wanted, Tunnel of Love, and Seismic Activity
- **Shared event pool** — 40 items obtainable from any of the new public events
- **Prize terminal items** — redeemed with Nuka-K points across five reward levels
- **Vendor purchases** — outfits and balloon plans sold by Betty Hill and the robot Chloe

<Callout type="warning" title="Before You Farm">
Some rewards were datamined from the Public Test Server (PTS). Individual items and drop behavior may be adjusted before or after the live release. Character-locked rewards are non-tradable, so plan your farming around the characters you actually play.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Event Reward Breakdown">

Each public event carries its own reward pool, and Most Wanted is by far the largest with 21 potential drops. Here is how the four events compare:

| Event | Location | Reward Count | Notable Unique Weapons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spin the Wheel | Big Top tent | 14 | Wacker Smacker (Super Sledge) |
| Most Wanted | Wild West Showdown | 21 | Gunter's Big Iron, Western Spirit |
| Tunnel of Love | Tunnel of Love area | 5 | Love Tap, Burning Love |
| Seismic Activity | Ultrasite Titan arena | 10 | Mole Rat Bat, The Grand Finale |

**Spin the Wheel** runs five unique rounds inside the Big Top tent and awards circus-themed plans such as the bottle plushie, Cappy plushie, circus bench, stilts, and the Seesaw, alongside the Wacker Smacker execution Super Sledge.

**Most Wanted** is the farming highlight. Beyond the massive Western mural set and the popular Nuka-World Cowboy duster and hat, it offers two standout guns:

- **Gunter's Big Iron** — a three-star Anti-Armor Western revolver converted to .45 ammo
- **Western Spirit** — a Furious lever action rifle with two extra rounds of capacity

**Tunnel of Love** has the smallest pool but still holds two weapons worth grabbing: the **Love Tap** (a .45 submachine gun) and **Burning Love**, an Assassin's prefix compound bow whose arrows deal fire damage.

**Seismic Activity** pits you against the Ultrasite Titan and rewards plans for the desktop Nuka launcher, the large Ultrasite shard (big enough to fill a foundation), a Titan plush and backpack flair, plus **The Grand Finale** — a two-shot Broadsider with explosive fireworks effects and a 10 percent damage increase.

<Callout type="tip" title="Farming Priority">
Target Most Wanted first for the widest reward table, then Seismic Activity for The Grand Finale. Tunnel of Love's Burning Love is a top pick for archer builds despite the small pool.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Prize Terminal: Five Reward Levels">

The Nuka-K Prize Terminal is the long-term grind. You earn **Nuka-K points** by participating in the new events and playing arcade games like Bandit Roundup and Wakkomi inside the Nuka tent, then spend those points across five escalating reward levels.

| Level | Key Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Standard common rewards |
| Level 2 | Mutated goldfish plan, Nuka-Cade snow globe, Nuka-World on Tour shirt |
| Level 3 | First Zapper, Nuka-World jumpsuit, Nuka-Cola gun ammo |
| Level 4 | Speakers and ukulele plans (incl. Quantum ukulele), Cherry/Quantum gun ammo |
| Level 5 | All four arcade game plans, weaponized Nuka-Cola schematics |

The First Zapper is the crown jewel of this system. At level 3 it is underwhelming, but the **level 5 weaponized Nuka-Cola schematics** unlock free mods that dramatically boost its damage:

  <Card title="Cherry Gun Mod">
    - Fires **explosive Nuka-Cherry** rounds
    - Weapon stays at level 1
    - Solid mid-tier damage
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nuka-Cola Gun Mod">
    - Fires **radiated Nuka-Cola**
    - Good for radiation builds
    - Ammo from level 3 terminal
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quantum Gun Mod">
    - **Explosive Quantum damage**
    - Highest damage of the three mods
    - Ammo from level 4 terminal
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Callout type="success" title="Endgame Payoff">
Redeeming the level 5 plans lets you build Bottle Blaster, Nuka Zappa Race, Wakkomi, and Bandit Roundup at your own camp — and the Quantum mod turns the First Zapper into a genuinely strong weapon.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Map} title="Shared Pool, Vendors & Camp Rewards">

The shared event pool contains **40 items** obtainable from any of the new events, making it the easiest way to passively accumulate camp decorations. Highlights include:

- Four different bottle and Cappy statues
- Two backpack flaires and the small bottle wind-up robot
- The Cappy Clapper and Cappy Smasher Super Sledge paint
- Nuclear bottle kiosks with **six variations**
- Nuka-Cola stalls in Original, Quantum, Cherry, and Grape variants
- Matching sets of four Nuka-Cola balloons and eight ad barriers

Two vendors round out the collection: **Betty Hill**, a ghoul working at the tour, sells outfit sets including the Bottling Cappy jacket and jeans, the Nuka-Curler set, and the orange Nuka-World jacket. The robot **Chloe** sells plans for the Cappy and bottle balloons.

| Reward Source | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| Shared event pool | Camp builders, decorations |
| Betty Hill | Cosmetic outfits |
| Chloe | Balloon camp plans |
| Prize terminal | Arcade games, First Zapper mods |
| Most Wanted | Unique weapons, Western builds |

<Callout type="info" title="Size Reference">
The Western mural set and Wild West entrance sign are enormous — roughly person-height. The Nuka-Cola ad barriers are surprisingly small by comparison. Check placements before committing scrap.
</Callout>

</Section>
