<Section icon={Trophy} title="How Country Ranking Works in World of Nukes">

In World of Nukes, every player develops a private island into a military nation. The unofficial **country ranking** players talk about reflects how far a nation has progressed across four pillars: economic output, construction tier, offensive capability, and defensive coverage. There is no single published leaderboard from the developer, so the community judges country strength by what a nation can build, launch, and survive.

The progression from a starting camp to a superpower follows recognizable tiers. Understanding where your country sits on that curve tells you exactly what to build next instead of wasting resources on the wrong upgrades.

| Tier | Country Stage | Defining Features | Rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Starting Camp | Basic camp, minimal income, no missiles | ★ |
| Tier 2 | Developing Nation | Steady income buildings, first anti-air defenses | ★★ |
| Tier 3 | Regional Power | Missile systems online, layered defense, early vehicles | ★★★ |
| Tier 4 | Major Power | Jets and warships in regular operation, upgraded core | ★★★★ |
| Tier 5 | Superpower | Wonders under construction, Doomsday Device progress | ★★★★★ |

<Callout type="tip" title="Ranking Is Dynamic">
Servers hold up to **7 players**, so your standing changes as rivals grow or get wiped. Re-scout enemy islands before every major attack to confirm their current tier.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Climbing the Country Rankings">

Follow this progression route to move up the **World of Nukes country ranking** ladder efficiently, from a first camp to superpower status.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish a Stable Camp">
    Place your starting camp with a compact layout. Prioritize resource-generating structures and keep the footprint tight so later expansion stays efficient. Secure basic income before investing in any military systems.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Grow the National Economy">
    Upgrade income-producing buildings before optional structures. Reinvest earnings into long-term growth. A stable economy lets you purchase vehicles, recover from attacks, and maintain steady development.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Expand Infrastructure in Layers">
    Build outward in organized patterns. Reserve open space for missile systems, anti-air defenses, and shields. Keep key buildings protected and accessible as the country grows.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Deploy Missiles and Early Defenses">
    Construct missile infrastructure when resources allow, and learn the launch interface before committing to attacks. Place anti-air around important buildings and use shields to protect the island core.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Field Jets, Warships, and Advanced Projects">
    Add aircraft for rapid strikes and warships for sustained naval pressure. With income and defenses secured, direct resources toward wonders and the Doomsday Device to reach the top tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Balance Checklist">
Before launching any major offensive, confirm your economy can fund a rebuild, your core is shielded, and a defensive response force of jets or warships remains at home.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Military Strength Comparison: What Raises Your Rank Fastest">

Offensive capability is the most visible ranking signal, but each military option serves a different strategic purpose.

| Military Asset | Role | Strength | Best Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Missile Launch System | **Deliberate strikes** | Confirmed range and resource cost | High-value enemy structures |
| Nuclear Attack System | Decisive pressure | Island-wide attack impact | Developed rival nations |
| Jets | Fast air raids | **Speed and mobility** | Exposed, lightly defended areas |
| Warships | Naval pressure | Durable, sustained fire | Coastal targets, sieges |
| Combined Assault | Total pressure | Air and naval coordination | Tier 4-5 superpowers |

Use jets to control distance from enemy defenses and reposition before opponents respond. Use warships to maintain pressure around enemy islands and support air operations. Reserve nuclear strikes for moments when a single decisive attack creates a lasting advantage.

<Callout type="tip" title="Scout Before You Strike">
Review the enemy island before launching. Prioritize **economic and strategic structures**, and never commit every vehicle offensively — an undefended home island invites immediate retaliation.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Defensive Standing: Surviving at the Top">

Ranking is not only about what you can destroy. Countries that survive repeated attacks hold their tier longer and waste fewer resources on rebuilding.

- **Anti-air defense**: position coverage over the central base, resource structures, and likely attack paths.
- **Island shields**: keep the shield close to the most valuable section of the island and build the surrounding layout around its protection.
- **Layered layout**: inner protection for the core, outer anti-air for exposed areas, and reserved space for future upgrades.
- **Gamepass support**: Early Warning Radar improves threat awareness, and Quick Rebuild reduces recovery time after attacks.

| Defense Layer | Protects | Placement Rule | Value at High Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anti-Air | Structures, attack routes | Near core and likely approach paths | ★★★★★ |
| Shield | **Island core** | Close to highest-value buildings | ★★★★★ |
| Layered Layout | Everything | Inner core, outer coverage | ★★★★ |
| Response Force | Key buildings | Jets and warships held at home | ★★★★ |

<Callout type="warning" title="High Rank Means High Risk">
Once wonders or the **Doomsday Device** are under construction, your island becomes one of the most attractive targets on the server. Upgrade defensive capacity before creating high-value structures.
</Callout>

</Section>
