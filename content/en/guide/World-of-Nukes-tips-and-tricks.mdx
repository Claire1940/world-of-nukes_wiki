<Section icon={Map} title="Core World of Nukes Strategy Fundamentals">

World of Nukes is a Roblox nuclear warfare strategy tycoon where you build an island, launch missiles, fly jets, and command warships against rival players. Success comes from balancing four pillars: economy, construction, offense, and defense. Players who overspend on military early tend to stall, while those who build a stable resource base first can sustain attacks and recover from losses.

The most reliable progression order is: economy first, infrastructure second, defense third, offense last. This mirrors how tycoon progression works — income funds everything else.

<Callout type="tip" title="Golden Rule">
Treat missile deployment as a strategic decision, not an automatic action. Confirm range, availability, and resource cost before every launch.
</Callout>

**Priority comparison for early-game decisions:**

| Decision | Recommended Priority | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Income buildings | ★★★★★ | Funds all construction and recovery |
| Compact island layout | ★★★★ | Keeps expansion efficient later |
| Anti-air placement | ★★★★ | Protects economy from early raids |
| Missile systems | ★★★ | Only after economy is stable |
| Wonders | ★★ | Endgame project, needs huge resources |

  <Card title="Island Shield">

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Never extend defenses to unimportant outer areas before your island core is covered. Losing your economy to one strike sets you back more than any peripheral damage.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Offense: Missiles, Jets, and Warships">

Offensive power depends on your island economy and target selection. Review the enemy island before launching, prioritize valuable structures, and avoid attacks that leave your own island undefended.

**Attack system roles:**

| System | Role | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| Missile Launch System | Deliberate strikes | Confirmed high-value targets |
| Nuclear Attack System | Large-scale pressure | Decisive moments against developed rivals |
| Jets | Fast air attacks | Rapid strikes on exposed areas |
| Warships | Sustained naval pressure | Coastal bombardment and support |

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Scout the Target">
    Fly a jet or position a warship near the enemy island. Identify economic and strategic structures worth hitting, and note defensive coverage.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Pick the Attack Angle">
    Use jets for speed and warships for sustained pressure. Attacking from multiple angles creates openings instead of feeding units into defenses.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Launch the Strike">
    Confirm weapon availability and resource cost, then commit. Prioritize economic structures to weaken the rival's long-term position.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Withdraw and Recover">
    Return vehicles to safe positions, rebuild losses, and shore up your own defenses before the next operation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Combined Arms">
Island assaults work best when vehicle roles are combined — mobility from jets, pressure from warships, and missiles reserved for decisive strikes.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Endgame Progression and Economy Tricks">

Wonders and the Doomsday Device represent the highest level of island progression. Rushing them before your economy and defenses can support them creates a high-value target you cannot protect.

**Endgame preparation roadmap:**

| Stage | Objective | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reliable income | Upgrade income buildings before optional structures |
| 2 | Infrastructure | Unlock construction options, organize production |
| 3 | Military readiness | Add defenses and vehicle coverage |
| 4 | Advanced project | Commit resources to wonders or Doomsday Device |
| 5 | Protection | Guard high-value structures from enemy raids |

<Callout type="success" title="Pro Trick">
Reinvest earnings into buildings that improve long-term growth instead of stockpiling. A compounding economy outpaces rivals who spend everything on military assets too early.
</Callout>
