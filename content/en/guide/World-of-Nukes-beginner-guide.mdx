<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Your First Hour of Progression">

Follow this four-stage route to take a fresh camp to a defensible, missile-capable island. Each stage builds on the previous one, so resist the urge to skip ahead.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the First Camp">
    Place your initial camp and organize the available island space. Prioritize structures that generate and manage resources, and keep the first layout compact so later expansion stays efficient. A tight core is easier to defend and cheaper to rebuild.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Expand the Island">
    Use collected resources to construct additional buildings. Expand outward in an organized pattern instead of scattering structures, and deliberately reserve open space for missile systems, anti-air defenses, and shields. Plan the island around both production and protection.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Deploy Missile Systems">
    Build missile-related structures once resources allow. Learn the launch interface and enemy target selection mechanics before committing to an attack. Missiles are a strategic decision, not an automatic action, so avoid wasting limited offensive resources on low-value targets.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Early Defenses">
    Place anti-air defenses around important buildings and likely attack routes. Use shields to protect the island core from nuclear strikes, and keep improving the economy so defensive coverage can expand alongside your country.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Beginner Trap">
Do not spend your entire resource pool on missiles in the first hour. An undefended island that strikes first usually gets flattened in retaliation, losing the very economy that funded the attack.
</Callout>

</Section>

| Island Shield | Protects the core from nuclear strikes | Keep it close to your most valuable section |
| Layered Layout | Combines both systems | Inner protection for the core, outer anti-air for exposed areas |

<Section icon={Swords} title="Missiles, Jets, and Warships: When to Use Each">

Once your economy stabilizes, offensive options open up. Each attack system fills a distinct role, and combining them produces the best results against developed rivals.

| Attack System | Role | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| Missile Launch System | Core offensive strikes | Deliberate attacks on selected enemy targets after confirming range and cost |
| Nuclear Attack System | Large-scale strikes | Reserve for high-value targets and decisive moments |
| Jets | Fast air attack | Rapid strikes, mobile defense, hitting exposed structures |
| Warships | Naval pressure | Sustained coastal attacks and supporting island assaults |

**Assault tactics that work:**
- Scout the target island before committing your strongest vehicles
- Use jets for speed and warships for sustained pressure, not one or the other alone
- Prioritize economic and strategic structures during an assault
- Keep a defensive response force at home instead of sending everything out

<Callout type="info" title="Target Selection">
Review the enemy island before launching. A strike that removes a rival's income generation hurts far more over time than one that destroys cheap outer buildings, and it leaves your own island less exposed to counterattack.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Long-Term Goals: Wonders and the Doomsday Device">

Advanced progression in World of Nukes leads toward **wonders** and the **Doomsday Device**, the highest tier of island development. These are long-term objectives, not first-week projects.

The recommended order for endgame preparation:

| Stage | Objective | Key Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stable income | Upgrade income buildings before optional structures |
| 2 | Core infrastructure | Unlock additional construction and utility options |
| 3 | Defensive capacity | Upgrade anti-air and shields before creating high-value targets |
| 4 | Military readiness | Field jets and warships for defense and pressure |
| 5 | Advanced projects | Commit resources to wonders and the Doomsday Device |

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Rush the Endgame">
Starting a wonder or the Doomsday Device before your economy and defenses can support it creates a high-value target you cannot protect. Build the foundation first.
</Callout>
