<Section icon={Map} title="World of Nukes How to Play: The Basics">

World of Nukes how to play is the first question every new recruit asks when they drop into this war-focused Roblox experience. The game plays out like a persistent skirmish: you spawn onto an island battlefield divided between rival players, and almost immediately you're at war. Peace rarely lasts long, and the moment a rival turns "rebel," the shooting starts. Your job is simple in concept — destroy enemy assets, collect money, grab stashes, and grow strong enough that no one can touch you.

**Video Highlights:**
- Combat begins quickly — rival players declare "rebel" status and open fire almost immediately
- Destroying enemy structures and money generators converts directly into cash
- **Stashes** are high-value loot targets; stealing one can be worth millions
- **Air drops** land on the map and can be claimed for bonus rewards
- Late-game unlocks include guided missiles, destroyers, cruisers, and helicopters

<YouTubeEmbed videoId="hiexeO3Ftls" title="Playing World of Nukes in Roblox" />

The core economy revolves around destruction. Anything you destroy on the battlefield generates value, which means aggressive players who keep pressure on their neighbors snowball faster than passive ones. Meanwhile, defensive-minded players can stack their base with generators and uranium mines to print passive income — but that wealth paints a target on your back, as heavily-developed bases attract raiders hunting for a big stash payout.

<Callout type="tip" title="First Five Minutes">
Don't rush into fights before you understand the map. Spend your opening minutes locating your base, identifying the nearest rival, and checking where air drops tend to land. Knowing the terrain before the first shot is fired gives you a decisive early advantage.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Core Mechanics: Money, Stashes, and Destruction">

The three pillars of World of Nukes gameplay feed into each other in a loop. Destroy things to earn money, spend money on units and buildings, and use those units to steal even bigger payouts from enemy stashes.

| Mechanic | What It Does | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Destruction rewards** | Destroying enemy structures pays out cash | Primary income source for aggressive players |
| **Stashes** | Hidden/defended loot caches on enemy bases | Stealing one can net **millions** in one raid |
| **Money generators** | Buildings that produce passive income | Funds progression without constant fighting |
| **Uranium mines** | High-tier resource structures | Late-game wealth engine, very raid-worthy |
| **Air drops** | Random supply crates landing on the map | Free bonus rewards for whoever claims them first |
| **Bunkers** | Defensive structures, including cluster variants | Protects your assets from incoming raids |

<Callout type="warning" title="Your Wealth Makes You a Target">
The richest players attract the most attention. If your base stacks uranium mines and generators, expect coordinated raid attempts. Spread out critical structures instead of clustering everything in one spot — a clustered base can be wiped in a single missile run.
</Callout>

Positioning your buildings matters more than most beginners realize. Placing money-generating structures close together is convenient for you, but it's also convenient for a raider with a bunker cluster or guided missile. A distributed layout forces attackers to commit more time and resources to dismantle your economy piecemeal.

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Your First Successful Raid">

Raiding is where World of Nukes shines, and the difference between a failed raid and a stash heist comes down to preparation. Follow this sequence to convert your early-game income into a major payout.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Scout the Target">
    Identify a rival base with visible money structures or a suspected stash. Watch their activity pattern — raid when the defender is distracted or already fighting someone else.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Stockpile Units">
    Before committing, make sure you can afford combat units such as a **One Car** or **Last Stop**-class options, plus a bunker cluster for cover during the assault.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Open with Suppression">
    Use your opening units to pressure their defenses and force the defender to react. The goal is to make them spend money defending, draining their economy.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Break Through and Loot">
    Once their defenses collapse, locate the stash. Everything you destroy along the way adds to your payout, so don't skip damaged structures on the path in.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Extract and Reinforce">
    Grab the stash and immediately shore up your own base. Successful raiders become targets the moment their windfall becomes known.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Snowball Smart">
A successful stash theft can fund an entire tier of unit unlocks at once. Reinvest into economy buildings first, then combat units — a strong income base makes every future raid cheaper to launch.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Defense and Counter-Raid Strategy">

Offense gets the headlines, but defense keeps your millions safe. Every structure you protect is money a raider doesn't take.

| Threat | Countermeasure | Cost Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ground raid units** | Bunker clusters near generators | Low |
| **Guided missiles** | Spread out key structures | Free (layout) |
| **Naval bombardment** | Keep high-value assets inland | Free (layout) |
| **Helicopter raids** | Fast-response units on standby | Medium |
| **Coordinated teams** | Allied players covering each other | Social |

<Callout type="warning" title="The PC Problem">
High-value assets — like a well-stocked base — can motivate multiple players to team up against you for a shared massive payout. If you're the strongest player on the server, expect coalition attempts. Stay mobile and never leave your entire fortune in one undefended stash.
</Callout>

Premium supply drops deserve special attention. These rarer air drops deliver outsized rewards and often trigger scrambles between multiple players. If you have a helicopter unlocked, you're almost always the first to arrive — which frequently decides who claims the prize.
