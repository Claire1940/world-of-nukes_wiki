<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step War Strategy">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Grind Offline First">
    Farm money in quiet sessions before recording or warring. Off-camera grinding is how veterans unlock Stormbreakers and multiple Sunbursts without dying and losing efficiency. Aim for a large cash reserve — one billion is a solid benchmark.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Build Your Economy">
    Purchase a Fusion Reactor and Space Elevator to accelerate income. These structures compound your earnings and unlock top-tier units for the fights ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Set Up Defense">
    Place two bunkers and queue two Titans before doing anything aggressive. Titans shut down enemy strike fighters, which is the most common counter to an aggressive player.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Declare War Selectively">
    Pick targets you can actually loot. Avoid servers where multiple coordinated players are online — a full-server dogpile will overwhelm even strong defenses.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Manage the Counter-Attack">
    Once you win loudly, expect everyone to target you. Use your gunship for personal defense, keep Titans refreshed, and know when to bank your money and relocate rather than fight a losing battle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Key Insight">
Dying is expensive beyond the respawn: each death reduces your earning efficiency for a timed window. Staying alive in a war matters as much as winning it.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Server Selection and Survival Rules">

Where and when you fight matters as much as what you bring. Populated servers mean bigger loot pools but also faster retaliation. Empty or low-population servers are safer for grinding but yield fewer war targets.

| Server State | Best For | Risk Level | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low population | Grinding, building economy | Low | Farm weapons and cash here |
| Medium population | Balanced wars | Medium | Fight with hybrid build |
| High population | Big loot, big danger | High | Only enter with maxed defense |
| Hostile dogpile | Nothing good | Severe | Bank money, defend or leave |

<Callout type="info" title="Server Lag Note">
Crowded war servers often lag heavily, which can wreck missile timing and unit control. If your Longbow shots are not registering, switch servers before committing more money to a fight.
</Callout>

</Section>
