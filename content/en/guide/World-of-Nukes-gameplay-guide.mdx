<Section icon={Map} title="What Is World of Nukes? Core Gameplay Overview">

World of Nukes is a nuclear warfare strategy tycoon on Roblox developed by Build. Defend. Nuke. You build your own island, develop its economy, deploy missile systems, fly combat jets, command warships, construct anti-air defenses, and launch attacks against other players. Each server hosts up to 7 players, which keeps every match focused and competitive.

The core loop is straightforward: generate resources, construct and upgrade buildings, expand your territory, and invest the surplus into offense and defense. Players who balance all four elements consistently outperform those who rush into pure aggression.

<Callout type="tip" title="Understand the Loop">
Treat every session as a cycle: earn, build, defend, attack. Skipping the defend step is the most common reason new players lose progress overnight.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Beginner Progression: Your First Island">

A successful start depends on balancing expansion, construction, military preparation, and defense. Build a stable resource base first, then grow your island while preparing weapons and defensive systems before enemy attacks begin.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the First Camp">
    Place your initial camp and organize the available island space. Prioritize structures that generate and manage resources, and keep the first layout compact so later expansion stays efficient.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Expand the Island">
    Use collected resources to construct additional buildings in an organized outward pattern instead of scattering structures. Reserve open space for missile systems, anti-air defenses, and shields.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Deploy Missile Systems">
    Build missile-related structures once resources allow. Learn the launch interface before committing to an attack, and select enemy targets carefully so you never waste limited offensive resources.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Early Defenses">
    Place anti-air defenses around important buildings and likely attack routes. Use shields to protect the island core from nuclear strikes, and keep improving the economy so defensive coverage expands with your country.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Beginner Mistake">
Do not spend every resource on military assets early. Without a stable income base, you cannot recover from a single enemy strike or fund continued upgrades.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Defense Strategy: Protecting Your Island">

A strong defense protects your island's most important structures first and expands coverage as the country grows. Anti-air defenses respond to incoming attacks, while shields provide vital protection for the island core against nuclear strikes.

| Defense Layer | Role | Placement Advice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anti-Air Defense | Intercept incoming aerial attacks | Position near central base, resource structures, and likely attack paths |
| Island Shield | Protect the core from nuclear strikes | Keep close to the most valuable island section |
| Layered Layout | Combine systems for survival | Inner protection for the core, outer anti-air for exposed areas |

The layered approach reduces the risk of losing all important buildings to a single attack and lets your defensive network grow together with your country. The **Early Warning Radar** gamepass further improves threat awareness by giving you more time to prepare defenses before an attack lands.

<Callout type="success" title="Defense Priority Order">
Protect the island core first, then key economic buildings, and only extend coverage to less important outer structures once the essentials are safe.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Jets, Warships, and Island Assaults">

Jets and warships provide different approaches to island warfare. Air units offer speed and flexible attack angles, while naval forces apply sustained pressure from the surrounding waters.

<CardGrid cols={2}>
  <Card title="Jets">
    - **Fast air attack and mobility**
    - Control distance from enemy defenses
    - Strike exposed targets, then return to safety
    - Best for rapid strikes and mobile defense
  </Card>
  <Card title="Warships">
    - **Sustained naval pressure**
    - Maintain positioning around enemy islands
    - Coordinate ship fire with air operations
    - Best for coastal pressure and assault support
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

The most effective island assaults combine both roles. Scout the target before committing your strongest vehicles, use jets for speed and warships for sustained pressure, and prioritize economic and strategic structures during the assault. Keep a defensive response force at home instead of sending every vehicle on offense.

<Callout type="warning" title="Rebuild Before Re-Engaging">
Replace vehicle losses and repair damaged infrastructure before launching another major operation. Attacking while exposed invites counterattacks on your own island.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Endgame: Wonders, Doomsday Device, and Milestones">

Wonders and the Doomsday Device represent the highest level of island progression. Build your economy, infrastructure, and military strength first, then direct resources toward these advanced systems.

| Endgame System | What It Does | How to Prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wonders | Major advanced landmark structures for developed islands | Complete core development, stockpile resources, protect the construction zone |
| Doomsday Device | Late-game strategic objective beyond standard vehicles | Advance through higher-tier progression and maintain strong defenses |

<Callout type="tip" title="Do Not Rush Endgame Projects">
Advanced structures become high-value targets the moment you build them. Upgrade defensive capacity before creating anything worth attacking.
</Callout>
