<Section icon={Shield} title="Best Early-Game Build Order">

The opening minutes of a match decide most outcomes. A disciplined build order gives you income, information, and protection before the first serious threat arrives.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Secure Your Core Economy">
    Immediately allocate workers or income structures to your primary resource. Do not spend on units you cannot yet support. A strong first-minute economy compounds for the entire match.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Scout Early">
    Send a cheap scout or use reconnaissance tools to identify your neighbors' strategies. Knowing whether you face a rusher or a farmer determines your next three decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Layer Basic Defense">
    Invest in at least one layer of shields or defensive structures before expanding outward. This deters opportunistic early strikes.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Expand When Safe">
    Claim additional resource nodes only after your core is protected. Expansion without defense is a gift to aggressive opponents.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Transition to Offense">
    Once income outpaces upkeep, begin building your strike capability while maintaining defensive redundancy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Common Mistake">
Never leave your base undefended while attacking. A counterstrike during your offensive window is the most common way strong players lose winnable matches.
</Callout>

Recommended opening allocation:

| Category | Share of Early Resources | Notes |
|----------|--------------------------|-------|
| **Economy** | 50-60% | Compounds over the match |
| **Defense** | 20-30% | Enough to deter early rushes |
| **Intelligence** | 10% | Cheap but high value |
| **Offense** | 0-10% | Only punish obvious weaklings |

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Mid-Game Tactics and Strike Timing">

Once both sides have economies running, the match becomes a battle of timing. The core of World of Nukes how to win at this stage is recognizing the window when your opponent cannot respond effectively.

### When to Strike

| Signal | What It Means | Recommended Action |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Enemy is expanding fast | Their defense is thin | **Strike now**, target new nodes |
| Enemy just launched an attack | Home base is weakened | Counterattack immediately |
| Enemy shields are down | Vulnerability window opened | Commit your main force |
| Enemy stockpiles visibly | Preparing a big push | Fortify and trade efficiently |

### When to Hold

<Callout type="tip" title="Patience Wins">
If you cannot identify a clear vulnerability, do not force an attack. Holding a superior economy while defending costs you nothing and drains your opponent.
</Callout>

Mid-game priorities checklist:

- Keep income growing every wave or cycle
- Maintain **at least two defensive layers** on critical structures
- Track enemy resource counts when the game allows it
- Avoid trading your strike force for low-value targets
- Deny enemy expansion nodes when cheap to do so

</Section>
